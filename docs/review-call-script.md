# Review call script (30 minutes)

Goal: the client understands every number before they file, and signs off in writing. Share the screen with
`methodology.md` and the state files open.

## Before the call

- `checks.txt` is all PASS, and every "needs review" line is resolved or written into section 6 of the
  methodology.
- Spot-check three SKUs by hand from `working.csv`: units × weight per component, then the category totals.
- Compare to last year's filing if they have one. Explain any change over 20% in a state.

## On the call

1. **The headline (2 min).** "You owe reports in [states]. California comes to [X] lbs, mostly [top category]."
2. **What we counted (5 min).** Walk through section 1: the files, what was excluded and why. Ask: "Is there any
   channel or warehouse we haven't seen?" Missing channels are the most common error.
3. **Packaging (10 min).** Go SKU by SKU through section 3, biggest sellers first. Ask: "Does this match what
   actually ships? Anything changed during 2026?" A mid-year packaging change means splitting the year.
4. **California plastic (5 min).** Explain that each plastic piece is one component, so the count is [N].
5. **Judgment calls (5 min).** Read section 6 out loud. Anything about who the producer is goes to their counsel.
   We don't decide it.
6. **Filing (3 min).** They upload the files under their own CAA account by May 31. Offer to stay on while they
   upload the first one.

## After the call

- Email the final files and methodology with a written summary of any changes agreed on the call.
- Ask for written approval: "Please reply to confirm you've reviewed these reports and are filing them."
- Send the balance payment link.
- Two weeks later, ask how the filing went. Pilot clients: book the feedback call and ask about a quote.
