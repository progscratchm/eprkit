4. Checks run before delivery

  • Every California category with plastic components has both plastic weight and plastic count
  • Plastic component weight never exceeds total weight, or 50% of total weight in non-plastic categories
  • Weights rounded to two decimals and counts to whole numbers, as the portal requires
  • Every product shipped in the year is mapped to packaging; unmapped products are listed for you to confirm

5. Methodology (excerpt)

Units shipped per state were taken from the brand's 2025 Shopify order export, using the shipping address state, and exclude returns and cancelled orders. Each product was mapped to its primary and shipping packaging. Weights for stock packaging come from the EPR Kit packaging library, calculated from dimensions and material specifications and checked against published supplier weights; custom packaging was weighed by the brand. Per-order items (shipping label and packing slip) were applied once per order. Category assignments follow CalRecycle's 2026 covered material categories and CAA's Oregon and Colorado category lists.
