Terms of service

Effective November 26, 2026.

These terms apply to the report preparation service provided by EPR Kit ("we") to the brand that engages us ("you"). A written order or email confirming your engagement sets out the price, states and delivery date.

The service

We prepare packaging EPR report data from the information you provide, and review it with you. You submit your own reports under your own account with the producer responsibility organization, and you're responsible for what you submit.

Your information

Reports depend on the accuracy and completeness of the data you send us, including order data and packaging details. We'll tell you about gaps or assumptions we notice, and our methodology states the assumptions used.

Not legal advice

We're not a law firm and don't give legal advice, including on whether you are the producer of a product. Regulatory information on this site is provided for general guidance and may change.

Fees, deposit and refund

Clients pay a $750 deposit to book their filing and the balance on delivery, both through Stripe's secure checkout. If we don't deliver your reports by the delivery date in your order (May 15, 2027 for the 2027 filing season), we refund your deposit in full. Fees owed to producer responsibility organizations are separate and paid by you.

Our correction promise

If Circular Action Alliance or a state program questions or rejects data we prepared, we'll correct it and help you resubmit at no extra charge until that reporting year closes. If a fee or penalty is assessed against you because of an error in our work (and not because of incomplete or inaccurate information you gave us), we'll refund the fees you paid us for that filing season. This refund is the full extent of our responsibility for such errors.

Confidentiality

We keep your data confidential and use it only to provide the service, as described in our privacy policy.

Liability

Apart from the correction promise above, and to the extent the law allows, our total liability for any claim relating to the service is limited to the fees you paid us for that filing season, and we aren't liable for indirect or consequential losses, or for fees, fines or penalties assessed by regulators or producer responsibility organizations.

Changes and contact

We may update these terms; the version in effect when you engage us applies to that engagement. Questions: support@eprkit.com or by post to EPR Kit, 418 Broadway, Ste N, Albany, NY 12207.
