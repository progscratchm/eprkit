Privacy policy

Privacy policy

Effective November 26, 2026.

This policy explains how EPR Kit ("we") handles information. Questions: support@eprkit.com or by post to EPR Kit, 418 Broadway, Ste N, Albany, NY 12207.

What we collect

  • Contact details you send us by email or form: name, company, email, phone.
  • Client data you share to have reports prepared: product lists, order exports, packaging specifications and related business records.
  • Website usage: the free tools on this site run in your browser; what you type into them is not sent to us.

How we use it

To reply to you, to prepare your reports, and to run our business (billing, records). We don't sell personal information or client data, and we don't use client data to market to anyone.

Sharing

Only with service providers that help us operate (for example email, file storage and payment processing), under confidentiality obligations, or where the law requires it.

Retention and deletion

Client data is kept while we work together and for as long as needed to support your filings and answer questions about them. Ask us at any time and we'll delete it, unless the law requires us to keep it.

Security

Client files are stored in access-controlled accounts with two-factor authentication, and access is limited to the people preparing your reports.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal information by emailing us. California residents can make these requests under the CCPA.

Changes

We'll post any changes here with a new effective date.
