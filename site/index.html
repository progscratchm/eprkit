What the states actually ask for

Pounds of packaging, by material, by state.

Every report comes down to how much of each packaging material you shipped into the state last year. The hard part is getting there from your products and orders: each box, mailer, bottle and cap has to be weighed, classified and multiplied by units shipped.

StateReportWhat goes in it
CaliforniaAnnual supply reportTotal pounds in each of 95 material categories, plus the weight and count of plastic components, your brands, affiliates and a written methodology
Oregon, ColoradoAnnual supply reportPounds in about 60 material categories, from PET bottles to kraft paper void fill
Minnesota, Maryland, WashingtonSimplified reportPounds in 8 broad material categories

California's portal rejects reports that break its rules, for example plastic component weight above total weight, or more than two decimals. We check every report against those rules before you upload it.

FAQ

Who submits the report?

You do, under your own account with the producer responsibility organization (Circular Action Alliance in most states). We prepare everything and walk you through it, and in California you can add us as a reporting contact to enter the data for you.

What data do you need from me?

A product export and a full year of order exports with ship-to states, from Shopify, Amazon or your 3PL, plus photos or spec sheets for any custom packaging. Most brands already have all of it.

How accurate are the packaging weights?

Stock items come from our library, with weights calculated from dimensions and material specs and checked against published supplier weights. Custom items are measured or taken from your supplier's specs. Every weight in your report says where it came from.

What if I'm exempt?

Then we'll tell you on the first call and you won't pay anything. If you're close to a threshold, we'll show you the numbers so you can decide.

Do you handle Europe or Canada?

Not for the 2027 season. If you also sell into the EU, we can introduce you to a registration partner there.

How is my data handled?

Your exports are used only to prepare your reports, aren't shared with anyone, and are deleted on request.
