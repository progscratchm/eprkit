Oregon packaging EPR: are you exempt?

Last checked November 26, 2026.

Oregon's program has been charging producers since July 2025, through the Circular Action Alliance (CAA). Small producers are exempt, but the tests have details that catch growing brands.

The two exemption tests

TestExempt if
RevenueLess than $5 million in global gross revenue
VolumeLess than 1 metric ton (about 2,205 lbs) of covered packaging supplied into Oregon

Meeting either test is generally enough. Oregon is the only state that counts in metric tons.

The detail that catches brands: associated producers

Oregon adds together the revenue and packaging of associated producers, such as a parent company and its subsidiaries, before applying the tests. Two small sister brands can be exempt on their own and obligated together.

A quick way to estimate your tonnage

Multiply your orders into Oregon by the packaging weight per order. Oregon is about 1.3% of the US population, so a brand shipping 150,000 US orders a year with 0.6 lbs of packaging each sends roughly 1,100 lbs into Oregon, under the 1 metric ton line. If you are obligated

  • Join CAA and report annually; reports are due May 31 for the prior year
  • Report pounds in about 60 material categories (look them up)
  • Producers with $5 million to $10 million in revenue pay a uniform annual fee rather than per-material fees
  • Penalties can reach $25,000 per day
