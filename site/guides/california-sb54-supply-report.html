How to file California's SB 54 annual supply report

Last checked November 26, 2026.

California's packaging program launches on January 1, 2027. Each year, producers report the packaging they supplied into the state through the Circular Action Alliance (CAA) producer portal. Here is what the report asks for and how to get it right.

Who has to file

Generally the brand owner of a product sold into California, or the importer or first distributor when the brand owner isn't in the US. Producers with less than $1 million in California gross sales can apply for a small producer exemption; it isn't automatic.

What goes in the report

ScreenWhat you enter
ExemptionsAny CalRecycle exemptions you are seeking, such as specific materials or health and safety
Supplied weightsFor each of 95 categories: total weight in pounds, weight of plastic components in pounds, and number of plastic components
BrandsEvery brand covered by the report
AffiliatesParent companies or subsidiaries that also supply packaging into California: EIN, legal name, and whether their pounds are in your report
MethodologyA written description of your data sources, calculation methods and assumptions
SubmissionRequires a signed California Interim State Addendum; only the primary contact or authorized representative can submit, and the report locks once submitted

The rules the portal enforces

  • Weights to at most two decimal places; component counts as whole numbers
  • For any category "with plastic component" that has weight, plastic weight and plastic count are required
  • Plastic component weight can't exceed total weight
  • In non-plastic categories, plastic component weight can't exceed 50% of total weight

How the categories work

Categories combine material, form and whether a non-plastic item has a plastic part. Cardboard alone is 25_PF9N; cardboard with a plastic component is 25_PF9P. Glass, metal, paper and wood come in these "with" and "without" pairs; plastics are split by resin and form, such as PET bottles or LDPE film. Items with two or more sides of 2 inches or less have their own "small" categories. Look up a category.

Preparing your data

  1. Export a full calendar year of orders with ship-to states, from every channel.
  2. List the packaging for each product, including shipping packaging and per-order items like labels.
  3. Get a weight for each packaging item, from a scale, your supplier's spec, or a reference library.
  4. Assign each item a category, then multiply by units shipped into California.
  5. Check the totals against the rules above and write down how you did it.

Key dates

  • January 1, 2027: program launches; producers who aren't compliant can be barred from selling covered packaging in California
  • Early 2027: first fee invoices, with rates that depend partly on recyclability
  • May 31, 2027: annual supply report for 2026 data
