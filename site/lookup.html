Which category is it?

Type a packaging item or material, such as "poly mailer", "kraft", "PET bottle" or "pouch". You'll see matching items from our packaging library with their California and Oregon/Colorado categories, and the matching official categories.

California: CalRecycle 2026 covered material categories (95). Oregon/Colorado: CAA reporting categories (60), from the Oregon program plan; confirm against the current list in the CAA portal. Category choice can depend on details such as attached plastic parts, so treat results as a starting point.
