**Testing instructions (for the reviewer)**

1. Install EPR Kit on your test store. It opens inside the Shopify admin and connects with read-only access to orders and products. No separate login.
2. Step 1 shows "Connected" and starts pulling the store's 2026 orders and products in the background (a few minutes for a small store; the page refreshes itself).
3. Click "Book my 2027 reports" to approve the one-time charge. On development stores it's a test charge. After approval you return to the app, step 2 shows "Booked", and step 3 links to the client upload page.
4. The service itself (packaging mapping and report preparation) is delivered by the EPR Kit team after booking; no further action in the app is needed for testing.
5. Uninstalling removes our access token immediately (app/uninstalled webhook); shop/redact deletes the store's exported data.
